# 90-second demo script

Prerequisites: backend on `:8000`, frontend on `:3000`, no API keys required.

1. **0:00 Open dashboard** — http://localhost:3000. Point to **DEMO REPLAY MODE** and **Evidence Engine**. Six metric tiles, India-centered dark map.  
2. **0:08 Explain one hotspot** — “These are clustered thermal observations, not confirmed fires.”  
3. **0:15 Click source** — In the explorer, open **Persistent refinery thermal source** (Jamnagar).  
4. **0:22 Industrial context** — Investigation → Nearby facilities: distance in metres, counts within 1/2 km, language “spatial association, not causation.”  
5. **0:32 Temporal persistence** — 7/14/30/90-day counts, active days, night-weighted detections.  
6. **0:40 Evidence** — Supporting cards with actual FRP, stability, facility distance.  
7. **0:48 Counter-evidence** — Scroll to “What could be wrong” / Why-not wildfire.  
8. **0:55 Classification** — Persistent Industrial Thermal Source, badge PERS (not ABN).  
9. **1:00 Confidence** — Band HIGH or MEDIUM; no fake percentage.  
10. **1:08 Analyst review** — Navigate to Review. Confirm or add a note; audit trail updates.  
11. **1:18 Replay** — Replay page: play/pause/speed/slider. Observation counts change with date.  
12. **1:28 Prioritized sources** — Sort explorer by priority; mention the Visakhapatnam abnormal *candidate* and the unresolved Rajasthan hotspot. Stop on: “The analyst still decides.”

Do not say LIVE. Do not claim model accuracy.
